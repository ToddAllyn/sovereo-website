How this is calculated. Each country's cost of living is measured by its price level, the World Bank purchasing-power-parity conversion factor divided by the current market exchange rate, which shows how expensive a comparable basket of goods and services is relative to the United States. Comparing two countries gives the relative cost of the same lifestyle in each, and applying that ratio to your budget shows what you would need to maintain it. Purchasing-power factors are from the World Bank (2021 base, inflation-adjusted); exchange rates are fetched live. These are country-level estimates for orientation, not city-level prices or a personal budget, and all figures are best estimates rather than exact facts.
