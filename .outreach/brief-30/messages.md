# Message bank

## Rules that override everything below

1. These are frames, not scripts. Every message that goes out is rewritten for
   one person. Nothing identical is ever sent twice.
2. Every `[bracket]` must be filled with something real from their profile. If a
   bracket cannot be filled, that person is not ready to message. Skip them.
3. No em dashes. Commas, periods, colons, parentheses.
4. One ask per message. One question mark, not three.
5. Open with their situation, never with Todd.
6. Never send the Substack link before they say yes to it.
7. One follow-up per person, ever. Then stop.

Rotate the variants. Three openers per track, so twenty messages a day never
read as one message sent twenty times.

---

## The insight bank

Message two has to carry one specific, checkable thing they did not know. Pull
this week's from the current Brief. Rotate so the same line is not reused across
a cohort. Examples of the right shape and specificity:

- The income bar on most residency visas is indexed and reset annually, so the
  number you qualified against in your research is not the number you will apply
  against next year.
- Portugal's IFICI is not NHR renamed. The eligibility gate moved, and pension
  income is treated differently under it.
- A pension paid in dollars into a euro cost base is a currency position you did
  not choose to open. Most people model the cost of living and never model that.
- Brent about 40 percent off the wartime high, gold under $4,000, and yuan
  settlement sliding on the CIPS rail were one event, not three. The correlation
  is the signal.
- The cheapest month to move is set by the lease cycle in the destination city,
  not by the flight price.

Swap in the current week's. A stale insight reads like a template.

## Track A, warm direct. Three messages.

For people who already commented, reacted, viewed the profile, or read the SITREP.

### A1, the opener. No pitch. Earn the second reply.

Variant 1, they engaged a post
> [First], you left a note on the [specific topic] post last week and it was a
> sharper read than most. Quick one since you are closer to this than the average
> reader: are you weighing a move yourself, or watching the money side of it from
> where you already are?

Variant 2, they already live abroad
> [First], noticed you have been in [city] since [year]. Most of what gets written
> about that move is written by people who never made it. Curious what caught you
> out that the research did not warn you about.

Variant 3, they are on the free SITREP list
> [First], thanks for reading the SITREP. You are in the group that actually opens
> it, which is rarer than you would think. Quick question so I write it better:
> what are you trying to decide right now, the country, the money, or the timing?

### A2, value first. Still no pitch.

> That tracks with what I am seeing. The part that catches most people at that
> stage is [one insight from the bank, matched to what they just told you].
>
> I ran the full version of that in this week's Brief. Want me to send you the
> issue?

Wait for a yes. Then send Issue #009 or the current issue as a link, with one
line, not a paragraph:

> Here it is. The clock section is the part that matters for your situation.

### A3, the offer. Only after they have read it.

> Glad it was useful.
>
> The Brief runs weekly. One catalyst, the dated action, and a falsifiable call
> that gets marked correct or wrong in public on the Reckoning ledger. Every past
> call is sitting there, including the misses, so you can audit the record before
> you trust a new one.
>
> $470 for the year, which is two months free against monthly. Billing runs on
> Substack, cancel in two clicks.
>
> Want the link?

If they hesitate on price, and only then:

> There is a monthly at $47 if you would rather test it for a few weeks first.
> Same issues either way.

## Track B, qualified followers. Four messages, two step.

For followers with means or intent in the profile and no engagement yet.

### B1, pain first, tease, invite. Day 0.

Variant 1, they moved recently
> [First], saw you moved to [city] in [year]. The thing that gets most people at
> the two year mark is [insight]. I track this daily for exactly that decision.
> Want the current read on [country]?

Variant 2, cross-border role
> [First], you run [region] for [company], so you already watch more of this than
> most people do. The piece that is moving right now and getting almost no
> coverage is [insight]. Happy to send you the read on it if it is useful.

Variant 3, pre-retirement with assets
> [First], you have been at [company] [number] years, so retirement is a real
> calculation rather than a someday. The number almost nobody runs is [insight].
> Want me to send you what it looks like on current figures?

### B2, deliver. No ask at all.

> Here it is. [One or two sentences on what is in it and why it matters for them.]
> No need to reply, just thought it was relevant to where you are.

That last line is the one that gets the reply. Leave it in.

### B3, the bridge. Day 7 to 10.

> [First], [specific thing that changed since B2, a number, a date, a rule]. It
> moved the read I sent you.
>
> Are you still looking at [country], or has that shifted?

### B4, the offer. Day 14, only if B3 got a reply.

> Makes sense. Here is the honest version of what I do.
>
> The free SITREP tells you what moved. The Brief tells you what to do about it,
> one dated call a week, scored in public so the record is auditable. It is built
> for exactly the decision you are making.
>
> $470 for the year, two months free against the monthly. Cancel in two clicks.
>
> Want the link, or would you rather keep reading the free one for a while?

That second option is deliberate. It gives them a way to say no that is not the
end of the relationship, and a meaningful share of them take the paid one instead
precisely because the exit was offered.

### If no reply to B1, one close-out only. Day 21.

> [First], I will leave it here so I am not cluttering your inbox. The free daily
> is at mail.sovereo.com if it ever becomes useful. Good luck with [their thing].

Then mark NO_REPLY and never message again.

## The founding member ask. Top five names only.

Send this instead of the standard close, and only to someone who has already
replied substantively twice.

> [First], I am opening a small founding group on the Brief and you are on the
> short list for it, so I would rather ask you directly than put it in a post.
>
> It is $750 for the year. Everything in the annual, plus a direct line to the
> desk when you need something run against your own numbers, and your name on the
> founding roll.
>
> The reason I am asking you specifically is [genuine, specific reason tied to
> their situation or their contribution].
>
> Worth a conversation?

Never send this to someone who has not already engaged twice. It reads as a
price increase rather than an invitation.

## Before every send, check

- Could this message have been sent to anyone else on the list? If yes, rewrite it.
- Does it open with them, not with Todd?
- One ask, one question mark?
- Any em dash, any "here is the thing", any rule of three, any "landscape" or
  "navigate" or "crucial"? Cut it.
- Is the link held back until they said yes?
