# Qualification rubric

Paste a person's headline, location, current role, and their last post or comment.
Score them. Only 6 and above gets a message. Everything below stays on content.

## Score

**Means, 0 to 4**
- 4: investor, fund, family office, C-level, founder with a funded or exited company, partner, MD
- 3: senior director, VP, owner of a real business, physician, attorney, tenured academic
- 2: mid-career professional, senior engineer, consultant, long-tenured manager
- 1: early career, student, job seeking
- 0: no signal in the profile at all

**Intent, 0 to 4**
- 4: posted in the last 90 days about leaving, retiring abroad, a visa, a specific country, or asked a relocation question
- 3: already living outside their passport country, or moved in the last three years
- 2: cross-border role, LATAM or EMEA or APAC desk, remote-first, nomad in the bio
- 1: follows the space, engages with relocation or macro content generally
- 0: no signal

**Warmth, 0 to 3**
- 3: already on the free SITREP list, or has DMed before
- 2: commented on a post in the last 90 days
- 1: reacted to a post, viewed the profile, or newly followed
- 0: follower only, silent

## Routing

| Total | Warmth | Track | Sequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 or more | 2 or 3 | A | Warm direct, three messages |
| 6 or 7 | 2 or 3 | A | Warm direct, three messages |
| 6 or more | 0 or 1 | B | Two step, four messages |
| 6 or more | any, posted about moving | C | Cold outbound, connect first |
| 5 or less | any | none | Content only. Do not message. |

Top five by total score across the whole list get the founding member ask at
$750 in place of the standard annual close.

## Hard exclusions

Do not message, whatever the score:
- Anyone selling relocation, visa, or offshore services. Competitor, not a buyer.
- Recruiters and business development staff prospecting the same way.
- Anyone who has asked not to be contacted. Mark DNC in the tracker, permanently.
- Anyone whose profile signals financial distress. Wrong product, wrong moment.

## The signal that beats every score

Someone who asked a public question about a specific country in the last 30 days
is worth more than a fund partner with no intent. Intent outranks means. A person
weighing a real move will pay $470 to stop guessing. A wealthy person who is not
moving will not pay $47.
