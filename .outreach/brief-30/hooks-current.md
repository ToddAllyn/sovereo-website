## The rule on numbers

Use the number exactly as the SITREP states it, with its date. Never round it up,
never drop the date, never attribute it to a source that did not publish it. The
entire proposition is that this desk is auditable. One inflated figure in a cold
DM undoes the Reckoning ledger.

If you are not certain of a number, use the mechanism instead of the figure. "Both
Saudi export routes are disrupted at the same time" needs no decimal place.

## What to say when they ask what to do about it

Do not answer it in the DM. That is the Brief. The DM ends at the door:

> mail.sovereo.com. The daily is free.

The decision, the dated trigger and the scored call are the paid product. Giving
the answer away in the message removes the reason to subscribe and, worse, gives
an unhedged read to someone whose situation you do not know.
