# Positioning. Read this before writing any message.

Every other file in this folder defers to this one. If a line of copy anywhere
contradicts this page, this page wins and the line gets rewritten.

## What Sovereo is

An intelligence research desk. Its own masthead says it plainly:

> Primary-source intelligence for cross-border operators.
> Global intelligence. Personal consequence. Decisions with integrity.

It tracks capital, energy, policy and security events, triangulates them across
state organs, independent wires and institutional publications, and publishes
what they mean for a decision, sourced and dated.

The product is not information. Plenty of places have information. The product is
**decision integrity**: every forward call carries a probability and a scoring
date, gets marked correct or wrong in public on the Reckoning ledger, and is
never withdrawn. The misses sit beside the hits. That auditable record is the
reason to trust the next call, and nothing else in the category offers it.

- **The SITREP**, free and daily. What moved and what it means.
- **The Brief**, paid and weekly. One catalyst, the mechanism, the dated action,
  a falsifiable forecast with its scoring date, and the executive checklist.
- **The Reckoning**, public. Every call, dated and scored, permanently.

## What Sovereo is not

It is not a relocation company. It is not an expat service, a visa consultancy,
an offshore outfit, or a moving-abroad brand. Relocation is one decision the
research informs. It is not the identity, and it is not even the largest slice of
the audience.

Copy that opens with moving abroad shrinks the audience to people who are
relocating and tells everyone else the desk is not for them. Most of the list is
not relocating. They are exposed.

## Who this is actually for

Cross-border operators. Anyone whose money, margin or position moves when
capital, energy, policy or security moves somewhere else.

The SITREP already does the targeting for you. Every issue carries a **Sector
Impact** table and an **Exposure** block scored across four lenses: Income,
Health, Lifestyle, Legacy. Whoever the issue marks HIGH is that week's outbound
list. Read it as a prospecting brief, because that is what it is.

From the 15 September issue, HIGH was agriculture, shipping and logistics, and
refining and fuel retail, with consumer goods and retail at MEDIUM.

| The decision | Who has it |
|---|---|
| Do I renegotiate contracts, hedge, or pre-buy inventory before this passes through | Operators with fuel, freight or input cost exposure |
| What is my counterparty and sovereign exposure right now | Investors, family offices, anyone holding across jurisdictions |
| Do I reprice, absorb, or hold through this | Executives with cross-border revenue or supply |
| What is my fixed income actually worth as this runs | Retirees and pre-retirees |
| Where do I build a life, and what does it cost in full | People weighing a relocation |

That last row is one of five. Write to the table, not to the row.

## The four exposure lenses

The SITREP scores every event across Income, Health, Lifestyle and Legacy. Use
the same four in outreach. They are how you connect a pipeline in Saudi Arabia to
a person in your inbox, which is the entire trick:

- **Income.** Margin, contracts, input costs, the paycheck and what it buys.
- **Health.** Cold chains, medical supply, access where they live.
- **Lifestyle.** Mortgages, refinancing, fuel, the monthly cost base.
- **Legacy.** The long horizon. What survives the decade.

A message that names the event and stops is news. A message that names the event
and then the lens it lands on is intelligence. Always take the second step.

## The founder line

One quiet line, used as credibility and never as the pitch:

> a career in institutional finance and higher education, now running the desk
> from outside the US

Deploy it when someone asks why they should trust the read, or when the specific
decision in front of them is one Todd has personally made. Never open with it,
never build the value proposition on it, and never claim a PhD.

The reason this rule exists: "I moved my family abroad" is a credential for one
row of the table above. Leading with it tells the other four rows to leave.

## The qualifying question

Wrong, because it presumes the relocation row:

> Are you weighing a move?

Right, because it lets them tell you which row they are in:

> What decision are you closest to making right now?
> Where does this actually land for you?
> What are you watching at the moment?

Ask an open one, then write to whatever they name.

## House style, non-negotiable

No em dashes. No AI linguistics. Neutral on politics, sharp on the hook.
Institution tone, not influencer. No fear selling, no fake urgency, no promises.
