# The note, and the silent connection DM

Read `positioning.md` first. Sovereo is an intelligence research desk, not a
relocation brand. Copy that opens with moving abroad shrinks the audience to the
one row of the table that is relocating and tells the other four to leave.

---

## The note

For a connection request. LinkedIn caps the invitation note at 300 characters.

> [First], [specific thing]. I run an intelligence desk covering cross-border
> capital, currency and policy risk. We publish what moved and what it means,
> daily, sourced and dated. Free, no pitch.

193 characters empty. Worst realistic fill lands at 253, so every version clears
the cap with room left.

What each part does:

- **[specific thing]** is the whole note. It is the only proof you read their
  profile, and it is what earns the accept. Never send this without it.
- **"intelligence desk"** sets the category correctly in four words. It is a
  research operation, not a service business, and senior people accept from
  research operations.
- **"sourced and dated"** is the discipline claim in three words. It separates
  the desk from every commentator in their feed.
- **"free, no pitch"** removes the only reason to decline. An invitation that
  smells like a sales sequence gets declined by exactly the people you want.

Do not put a link in an invitation note. Links suppress acceptance. The link goes
in the message you send the day they accept.

### The note carries no dates and no numbers

This is a general note. It goes out for months without a rewrite, so nothing in
it can go stale. No figures, no "as of Tuesday", no reference to a specific
event. A note built on a live number is wrong within the week and worse than
wrong if he sends it late.

Currency enters at message two, not message one. That is the right order anyway.
The note earns the accept. The dated, sourced number in the next message proves
the desk is actually running. Leading with the number spends the proof before
anyone has agreed to listen.

## When they never post

Most of the list will have no recent post. That does not make them cold, it makes
them quiet. They are already 1st degree, so they take a direct message with no
invitation, no 300 character cap, and no invite budget spent. In practice this is
a better list than the loud one: less competition in the inbox, and the people
carrying real exposure are usually the ones not broadcasting it.

What changes is where the personal detail comes from. Walk the ladder and stop at
the first rung that has something.

| Rung | Signal on the profile | The line to write |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Posted in the last 90 days | `saw your post on [their exact situation]` |
| 2 | Started a new role in the last 6 months | `you moved to [company] in [month]` |
| 3 | Long tenure | `[number] years at [company] puts the income question in range` |
| 4 | Cross-border role, region, or location | `you cover [region] for [company]` or `you have been in [city] since [year]` |
| 5 | Career arc visible across roles | `you went from [field] into [field], which is not a common path` |
| 6 | Shared context | `we both came out of [company or school]` |
| 7 | Nothing at all | The reactivation note below. It needs no personal detail. |

Rungs 2 through 6 all exist on a profile that has never posted once.

### The reactivation note, rung 7

> [First], we have been connected a while and I have never once used it for
> anything, which seems like a waste on my end.
>
> Short version of what I do now: I run an intelligence research desk. We take
> cross-border capital, currency and policy shifts and publish what they actually
> mean for a decision, sourced and dated, every morning. It is free and there is
> no pitch behind it.
>
> mail.sovereo.com if it is useful.
>
> What are you watching at the moment?

Why it works with no personal detail:

- It opens by naming the awkward thing instead of faking a closeness that is not
  there. Quiet people read pretend warmth instantly and delete it.
- It is genuinely true of the relationship, so it is not a template even though
  the words repeat. The dishonest version is claiming to have followed someone's
  work when you have not.
- The ask is a free daily, which costs them nothing to accept and nothing to
  ignore.
- The closing question is open and about them. It does not presume they are
  moving anywhere.

Vary the first line across the batch:

- `we connected back when I was still on the institutional side, and I have never actually used it for anything`
- `we have been connected for a while without ever talking, so here is me fixing that`
- `this is overdue given how long we have been connected`
- `I went through my connections this week and realized I have never told you what I actually do now`

## The founder line, and when to use it

> a career in institutional finance and higher education, now running the desk
> from outside the US

It is credibility, not the pitch. Use it when someone asks why they should trust
the read, or when the decision in front of them happens to be one Todd has made
himself. Never open a message with it. Never build the value on it.

The reason: it is a credential for one row of the audience table. Leading with it
tells the other four rows the desk is not for them.

---

## Check before you send

- Does it name an intelligence research desk, or does it sound like a relocation
  service? If the second, rewrite it.
- Is it free of dates and figures? The note is general and must still read
  correctly in three months. Numbers belong in message two.
- One specific detail that could only be about this person.
- Under 300 characters if it is an invitation note, counted, not estimated.
- No link in an invitation note.
- Names the free daily, never the price.
- Closing question is open, and does not presume they are moving.
- No em dash, no AI tells, neutral tone.

## The rule that keeps this off the spam line

A DM to a silent connection may repeat its middle paragraph. It may never repeat
its first line and its last line. The opening names something true about them or
about the connection, and the closing question is one they could actually answer.
Those two get written fresh every time.

## Volume

25 DMs a day to existing connections, 20 to 40 connection requests. The silent
list consumes no invitation budget, which makes it the cheapest track you have.

If a profile is so thin that even rung 6 has nothing, do not force it. That person
converts through the offer posts and a profile that sells, not through a DM.
