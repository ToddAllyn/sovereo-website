# The five objections, and the reply

Never argue. Answer in two or three sentences, then hand back a single question
or a single door. A long defensive reply loses a sale that a short honest one
would have closed.

## 1. "That is a lot for a newsletter."

> Fair. It is priced like a tool rather than a magazine, because it is one
> decision a week rather than a reading habit. If the annual does not pay for
> itself on the first call you act on, it is the wrong product for you and you
> should cancel it. That is a two click thing on Substack.
>
> Want to read a full issue first and judge it that way?

Always route price resistance to the sample, never to a discount. There is no
discount. The two months free on annual is the concession, and it is already in
the price.

## 2. "Is this financial advice?"

> No, and I would not want it read that way. It is educational decision
> intelligence built from named public sources. It is not legal, tax,
> immigration, or financial advice, and anything specific to your situation
> should be confirmed with a licensed professional in that jurisdiction.
>
> What it does is put the mechanism and the dated trigger in front of you so you
> are asking that professional the right question.

Answer this one exactly this way, every time. Do not soften it.

## 3. "I already read [publication]."

> Good, most of them are better than me on breadth. The Brief is not competing on
> coverage. It is one catalyst a week with a dated action and a forecast that gets
> marked correct or wrong in public.
>
> Genuine question: can you check what [publication] said about this twelve months
> ago and see whether it held?

The scored ledger is the only real moat. Use it here.

## 4. "Let me think about it."

> Of course. I will not chase you on it.
>
> If it helps, the annual and the monthly get the same issues, so the only
> difference is whether you want to test it or commit to it. Either way the free
> daily keeps coming.

Then stop. Do not follow up on this one. A person who says this and is chased
does not come back. A person who says this and is left alone sometimes does.

## 5. "Can you just tell me where to move?"

> I can tell you what the data says for your situation, which is not quite the
> same thing and is more useful. Where to move depends on your income source,
> your health needs, your legal standing, and what you are optimising for.
>
> If you want it run against your actual numbers rather than in general, I do a
> private 45 minute session for that. Otherwise the Brief will get you most of
> the way.

This is the one objection that is worth more than the subscription. It is a
consultation buyer at $197 and possibly a country report after it. Do not push
them down to $47.

## The reply that closes more than any of the above

When someone gives you a real detail about their situation, answer the detail
before you answer the objection. A person who feels read buys. A person who feels
handled does not.
