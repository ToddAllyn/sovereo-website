Before you subscribe

How is this different from the free SITREP?

The SITREP is daily awareness: what moved and what it means. The Brief is the weekly decision: the one catalyst that matters, the dated action, and a falsifiable call scored in public. Awareness is free. The decision is the Brief.

What do you mean by "scored in public"?

Every forward call enters the Reckoning ledger with a probability and a scoring date, and is marked correct or wrong on that date. Nothing is ever removed. The misses sit beside the hits. That discipline, not any single call, is the product.

Is this financial advice?

No. The Brief is educational decision intelligence built from named public sources. It is not legal, tax, immigration, or financial advice. Confirm specifics with a licensed professional in the relevant jurisdiction.
