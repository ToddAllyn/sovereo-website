# Sovereo Intelligence Research — 20 second intro

Source of truth: `research-standard.html` (verification, source independence,
contrary evidence, neutrality, commerce after analysis, public forecast ledger).

Target read: 20 seconds. 53 spoken words at roughly 2.8 words per second, with
three short pauses. Calm, institutional, unhurried. No music swell, no fear tone.

## Voiceover (record this)

Most people decide where to live on rumor.

Sovereo runs on a standard.

Two independent sources, or it does not print.

Every judgment tested against the strongest case that it is wrong.

Every forecast dated and scored in public, right or wrong.

No camp. No pitch before the analysis.

Global intelligence. Personal consequence.

## Notes

- Line 5 is the differentiator. Do not cut it for time. Misses stay on screen
  next to hits, that is the whole point of showing the ledger.
- Hold the last card two full beats so the URL is readable on mobile.
- Palette: ink #0E1A2B, navy #14233B, gold #C8A45C, paper #FBF8F1.
- Type: Fraunces for the wordmark and card lines, Inter for small labels.
