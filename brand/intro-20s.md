# Sovereo Intelligence Research — 20 second intro

Source of truth: `research-standard.html` (verification, source independence,
contrary evidence, neutrality, commerce after analysis, public forecast ledger).

Target read: 20 seconds at roughly 2.8 words per second, with short pauses.
Calm, institutional, unhurried. No music swell, no fear tone.

## Product placement rule

One spoken ask, one URL. The free daily SITREP is the ask because it is free and
it is the top of the ladder. The Brief is named or shown, never pitched, and it
is never a second click. Send everything to `sovereo.com` and let the page route
to the Brief. Two URLs on a two second card is the same as no URL.

Use Build A when this is a standing channel intro that runs on every upload. The
end card teaches both product names by repetition and no proof line is sacrificed.
Use Build B when the intro runs standalone (an ad, a pinned post, a cold audience)
and the viewer may never see a second card.

## Build A — standing channel intro (53 words, products on the card only)

Most people decide where to live on rumor.

Sovereo runs on a standard.

Two independent sources, or it does not print.

Every judgment tested against the strongest case that it is wrong.

Every forecast dated and scored in public, right or wrong.

No camp. No pitch before the analysis.

Global intelligence. Personal consequence.

## Build B — standalone promo (54 words, products spoken)

Most people pick a country on rumor.

Sovereo runs on a standard.

Two independent sources, or it does not print.

Every judgment tested against the case that it is wrong.

Forecasts dated and scored in public, right or wrong.

That standard ships daily as the SITREP, weekly as the Brief.

### What this costs

Build B buys the product names by spending the neutrality line. That is a real
loss on a cold audience, so only run it where the viewer sees no other card.
If the read runs long, cut "right or wrong" before cutting anything else.

## Notes

- The ledger line is the differentiator. Do not cut it for time in either build.
  Misses stay on screen next to hits, that is the whole point of showing it.
- Hold the last card two full beats so the URL is readable on mobile.
- Never show a price, a Substack paywall, or a consultation link in the intro.
  Commerce comes after the analysis, and the intro is before all of it.
- Palette: ink #0E1A2B, navy #14233B, gold #C8A45C, paper #FBF8F1.
- Type: Fraunces for the wordmark and card lines, Inter for small labels.
