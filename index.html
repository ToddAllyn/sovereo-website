The world is connected
An election in South America moves the price of a raw material, a company's profits, and what a family's money can buy. A computer chip rule in Taiwan affects your devices and your work. A closed oil shipping route at Hormuz affects your fuel costs and your savings.
You do not have to cross a border to be affected. World events cross borders to reach you. Every day Sovereo traces the line from the event to you, with at least half of the evidence from original, regional and local sources, not the big Western news agencies.

Why Sovereo is different

It sells no deals

Many firms that help people move or hold money abroad earn a fee when you buy residency, citizenship, or property, or take a referral. Sovereo takes none of those fees. So no sale decides whether our research supports moving, waiting, choosing another place, or staying home.

It runs on evidence

Every score comes from named public data and proven research, from the Whitehall studies to the WHO Commission on Social Determinants of Health. You can see our sources, our method, what is uncertain, and how sure we are.

It comes from real life

Built by an economist who moved to another country himself and sees local conditions from the inside. He turned that judgment into a tool you can use for your own life.
