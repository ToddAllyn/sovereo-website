The Problem Nobody Warns You About

The rate is running your retirement, whether you watch it or not.

You did the hard part. You built an income and moved somewhere you can enjoy it. But the money you count on now passes through an exchange rate before it reaches your grocery bill, your rent, and your healthcare. That rate moves for reasons that have nothing to do with you, and it can quietly hand you a raise or a pay cut every month.

"The Retiree's Currency Playbook shows you how to read that rate, understand what moves it, and build a budget that holds up when it turns against you."
