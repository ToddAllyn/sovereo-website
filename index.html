Why Sovereo is different

It brokers nothing

Many relocation and offshore firms earn fees when a residency, citizenship, property, or referral transaction closes. Sovereo receives none of those fees. Transaction completion therefore does not determine whether the research supports moving, waiting, choosing elsewhere, or staying home.

It runs on evidence

Every score traces to named public data and established research, from the Whitehall studies to the WHO Commission on Social Determinants of Health. Sources, method, uncertainty, and confidence remain visible to the reader.

It was lived, not theorized

Built by an economist who made the cross-border move himself and reads the ground from the inside, then turned that judgment into an instrument you can use for your own life.
