Methods · Corrections

Research standard

The rules governing what Sovereo publishes, how confidence is earned, and how the public record is corrected.

Intelligence is useful only when the reader can distinguish verified fact, reasoned assessment, uncertainty, and commercial interest.

Verification

Consequential claims require corroboration appropriate to their importance. A single-source claim is attributed and marked unconfirmed or withheld. Institutional data may establish what an institution measured or officially did, but not every interpretation of it.

Source independence

Multiple reports do not count as independent when they repeat the same original claim. State, commercial, institutional, specialist, regional, and independent sources are read against one another. No category receives automatic authority.

Consequence before novelty

A new announcement does not automatically outrank an older development. Continuing events remain material while their human, institutional, financial, or operating consequences expand, compound, or approach a measurable decision point.

Contrary evidence

Every central judgment is tested against the strongest case that it is wrong. Contrary sources remain visible. A factual report is not dismissed merely because an assessment finds it inconvenient.

Neutrality and symmetry

The same evidentiary standard applies to governments, companies, advocacy organizations, and individuals. Sovereo describes conduct, incentives, exposure, and cost without adopting a political camp's moral vocabulary.

Commerce after analysis

Commercial offers do not determine the reporting, confidence, or conclusion. Sovereo does not publish analysis about a transaction flow touched by an affiliated entity. Any invitation to subscribe or purchase appears after the analytical work.

Confidence and impact labels

HighMaterial consequences are already operating, evidence is strong, and the exposure is difficult or costly to reverse.
MediumThe transmission path is credible and relevant, but scale, timing, or implementation remains partly unresolved.
LowThe development is worth monitoring, but current evidence does not establish broad or immediate consequence.

Public record

Corrections

When a published fact is materially wrong, the edition is corrected with the date and substance of the change. Corrections do not silently erase the earlier record. Forecasts remain in the public ledger whether they resolve correctly or incorrectly.

Readers may submit a correction with the claim, edition date, and supporting source. A correction request is evidence to examine, not a guarantee that a judgment will change.
