How this is calculated. Your household income is adjusted for household size using the OECD square-root scale (income divided by the square root of the number of people), then converted into 2021 international dollars using World Bank purchasing-power-parity factors, so it reflects what your money actually buys, not just an exchange rate. That figure is placed on the global income distribution from the World Bank Poverty and Inequality Platform (2024, via Our World in Data), which covers about 97 percent of the world's population. The "at today's exchange rate" line uses live market rates and is shown for reference only; the ranking itself uses purchasing power. All figures are best estimates, not exact facts. World population taken as 8.2 billion.
