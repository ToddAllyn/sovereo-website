How to read this, in plain English

You do not need any finance background. Here is the whole thing, simply.

What is the "top marginal rate"?

It is the tax rate on the highest slice of income, not on your whole income. Most countries tax in bands: the first part of your income is taxed lightly, later parts more. This shows the rate on the top band. It is the clearest single number for comparing how heavily a country taxes higher earners.

Why the real rate can be higher

Some countries add local or regional income tax, social security payments, or extra charges on top of the national rate. So the true top rate in places like the United States, Canada, Switzerland, or the Nordic countries can be well above the national figure shown here.

Why it matters

Tax is one of the biggest costs you pay, year after year, and it varies between countries more than almost anything else. If your income is not tied to one place, you can legally keep far more of it depending on which country taxes you as a resident. Sovereo helps people think this through.

Where this comes from. Figures are the official ("statutory") top income tax rates, meaning the rate on the highest band, for 2025. They come from public lists by KPMG, PwC, and Trading Economics. They are national rates only. Local or state income taxes, social security payments, extra charges, and special schemes for foreigners or flat taxes can raise or lower what people actually pay. Rates also change with each government budget. This is a comparison of headline rates for general education, not tax advice. Confirm any figure with a licensed professional before acting.
