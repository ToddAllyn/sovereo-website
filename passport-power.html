What is a "visa-free destination"?

A place your passport lets you enter without arranging a visa before you travel, either with no visa at all or with one granted on arrival. The more of these a passport has, the more freely you can travel for holidays, for business, or to leave at short notice.

Why passports differ so much

Access is built on trust between governments, and on deals where each side lets the other in. Security, the economy, and diplomacy all shape it. Wealthy, stable countries tend to win broad access. Countries seen as higher risk face more closed doors, whoever the traveler is.

Why it matters

Passport strength affects how easily you can travel, do business, or live abroad. It is one reason people seek the right to live in a second country, or a second citizenship. It has real value, but it is only one part of any life decision.
