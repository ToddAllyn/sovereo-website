## Research tasks with the longest lead time

Start these on day one. Everything else can be drafted from material already in hand.

1. **Extend the notice ledger from nine rows to thirty.** Fifteen tracked jurisdictions, every instrument that binds a household, each row carrying publication date, date in force, interval in days and mechanism. This is the book's original asset and it cannot be compressed at the end.
2. **Measure noise in Italian elective residency decisions.** Consulate by consulate, effective minimum applied to comparable files. Kahneman's category has a measurement method and nobody in this market has run it. If it produces a number, it is a second original finding and it belongs in chapter 7.
3. **Re-pull every gazette entry already in Exhibit 5.** They were verified for Foundations. They are being republished under an author's name in a trade book and must be verified again, to the recompute standard.
4. **Reconcile the Pedigree document against Foundations Part IV.** Confirm the roster is thirty-six, confirm no thinker appears whose job is not stated, and confirm no job is stated that the text does not actually do.
5. **Open the reader's forecast ledger.** The dated calls that appear in chapter 20 must be written and timestamped well before publication, or the standard the book demands of others is not one it met itself.
